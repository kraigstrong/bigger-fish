Eat smaller fish. Dodge bigger ones. Win the food race.

Bigger Fish is a one-touch arcade game about the food chain, and how quickly it turns. Hold anywhere to rise, let go to fall, and swim into anything smaller than you. Every meal makes you bigger, so the fish that chased you a moment ago becomes your next target.

But you're not the only one eating. Every fish around you is growing too, and it only takes one bigger fish to end your run. Eat fast, and stay one size ahead.

FIVE WORLDS, 75 LEVELS
• Shallow Reef: bright water and a fast food chain.
• Jelly Bloom: bounce off jellyfish tops, dodge their stinging tentacles.
• Kelp Forest: fish hide as shadows in the kelp until you get close.
• Sunset Cove: boats pass overhead dangling baited hooks. Leave the bait.
• Midnight Zone: your headlamp only reaches so far, and every glowing lure could belong to something bigger.

Each world has ten levels, then a Deep End of five extra-hard ones for when you can't get enough.

• One-touch controls: hold to rise, let go to fall.
• A living food chain: every fish eats and grows, just like you.
• Short runs built for one more try.
• Plays offline. No ads, no accounts.
• Progress backs up to iCloud and follows you to your iPhone and iPad.

Free to try: all of Shallow Reef and the first levels of every other world. A one-time purchase opens every world and every Deep End.
