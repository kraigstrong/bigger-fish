# Bigger Fish sound credits

Copied from Math Reef for the arcade prototype; the shared audio system will be consolidated later.
These are Mixkit **sound effects**, covered by its commercial sound-effects license (including games).
No Mixkit music is used. Full source and preparation details remain in `MathReef/Sounds/CREDITS.md`.

- `gulp.caf`: Mixkit 2925, "Soap bubble sound". Used for eating and dome bounces.
  Source: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/2925/2925-preview.mp3
  Trimmed to 0.26 seconds, peak -1.5 dBFS.
- `wrong.caf`: Mixkit 240, "Failure arcade alert notification". Used for a lost run.
  Source: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/240/240-preview.mp3
  Trimmed to 0.70 seconds, peak -3.0 dBFS.
- `star.caf`: Mixkit 600, "Achievement bell". Used for a level clear.
  Source: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/600/600-preview.mp3
  Trimmed to 1.40 seconds, peak -1.5 dBFS.
